About the Creator

RadarView Beta Version 8 - Released September 13, 2023

Amelia Urquhart (born 2004) is a weather photographer and a meteorology student at the University of Oklahoma. She has been interested in meteorology as long as she can remember, and she has early memories of watching The Weather Channel's secondary cable station on which they played the radar loop and forecast for the Dallas-Fort Worth area on repeat. She has sheltered several times from severe storms and tornadoes, including the Rowlett, TX EF-4 of 2015; the Wylie, TX Hailstorm of 2016; and the North Dallas EF-3 of 2019. She has experienced a heat index of 115 °F and a wind chill of −22 °F.

She started to learn to program in Java in 2016, and began her journey in meteorological programming by making a program that displayed current GFS model data. This later evolved into a project which uploaded images of current model data from the HRRR, HRDPS, HRRR-AK, HRDPS-NORTH, and GFS for areas across the world.

She is a member of the Storm Chase Archive

team. Her main contribution is the radar and satellite maps, and she is working on additional features for the site.

Her scientific interests include tornado dynamics, RFD thermodynamics, winter weather precipitation type nowcasting, and mesoscale weather modeling. She is a senior in undergraduate meteorology program, and her interests are likely to evolve and expand. She is also a dedicated storm chaser and has tallied 18 tornado sightings, as well as 8 aurora sightings (as of January 23, 2026). She often posts her chase photos to her Twitter account.
